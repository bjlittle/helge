# Intent: <title>

## Problem

What cannot be done today, in the originator's own words. Who feels it, how
often, what it costs.

## Proposed outcome

What better looks like, stated as an outcome rather than a solution.

## Affected users and systems

People, teams, services and repositories the change touches.

## Constraints

Restrictions the design must respect: policy, data, compatibility, time.

## Open questions

Points nobody can settle yet, each with who could settle it.
